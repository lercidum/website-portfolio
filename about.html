About Me

I'm a Video Editor with a strong passion for Motion Design and visual storytelling. While my portfolio currently consists of personal projects, each one reflects my dedication to continuous learning, creativity, and self-improvement. Every project is an opportunity to challenge myself, explore new techniques, and expand my creative abilities.

I also plan to develop my skills in Sound Design, as I believe a good motion graphics are enhanced by thoughtfully crafted audio. My goal is to combine strong visuals with immersive sound to create more engaging and impactful experiences.

I aspire to be a professional Motion Designer who delivers high quality work that meets and exceeds client expectations. I am committed to continuously improving my craft and creating meaningful designs that effectively bring ideas to life.
