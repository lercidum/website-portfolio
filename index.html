Blender

I am still less proficient to using blender but I use it to combine my Motion Graphics After Effects Workflow. Blender is a freeware and is great for most who want to learn more about 3D.

Adobe Creative Suite

I am proficient to using Adobe Creative Suite like Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, Audition and Illustrator. I use AE and Illustrator all the time for most of my Motion Graphics Workflow and use Premiere for Finishing Touches or Video Edits.

Figma

I am less proficient to Figma, I am still getting used to transitioning from Illustrator to Figma. The UI is minimalistic and easier to understand than Illustrator. I mostly switch to Figma if I need to get things done quick.
